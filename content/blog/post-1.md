## Why Rosemary Works

Rosemary has been used for centuries in Ayurvedic and natural medicine for its scalp-stimulating, anti-inflammatory, and anti-fungal properties. When applied topically, it nourishes hair follicles, improves circulation, and supports stronger, thicker growth.

### Benefits of Rosemary Hair Spray

- Stimulates hair follicles for faster, thicker growth
- Improves blood circulation to the scalp
- Reduces dandruff, itchiness, and dryness
- Strengthens hair roots and reduces breakage
- Adds natural shine and volume without buildup

## How to Make Rosemary Hair Growth Spray

### Ingredients

- 2 cups water
- 2 tablespoons dried rosemary leaves (or a handful of fresh leaves)
- Optional: a few drops of rosemary, lavender, or peppermint essential oil

### Method

1. Boil 2 cups of water in a saucepan.
2. Add the rosemary leaves and simmer for 10–15 minutes.
3. Turn off the heat and let the mixture steep for 30 minutes.
4. Strain the liquid into a clean spray bottle.
5. Add 3–5 drops of essential oil for an extra boost, if desired.
6. Let it cool before using.

## How to Use

- Spray directly on the scalp once or twice a day.
- Massage gently for 1–2 minutes to boost absorption.
- Use on clean, damp, or dry hair.
- No need to rinse — it’s a leave-in treatment.
- For best results, use consistently for 3–4 weeks.

### Pro Tip

Store your rosemary spray in the refrigerator and use within 7–10 days. For a longer shelf life, brew a more concentrated version and add a few drops of natural preservative like vitamin E or aloe vera juice.

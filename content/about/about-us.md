Fitness Vibe is a wellness clinic founded by Dt. Niharika Singh that blends classical Ayurvedic wisdom with evidence-based modern nutrition. Our mission is to restore balance by addressing root causes of health issues and helping clients build sustainable, nourishing habits.

Our approach:

- Personalized plans that respect individual history and goals
- Focus on hormonal balance, fertility support, gut recovery, and weight management
- Blending dietary strategies with lifestyle and stress-management techniques

At Fitness Vibe we believe wellness is a journey. We guide clients toward lasting behavioural change and resilient health — one step at a time.
